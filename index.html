How it works

Type your e-gate time and tap Add. The app picks the latest DSO 2 bus that reaches EGHQ before it, and shows a backup bus.

Typing: 4 digits, e.g. 1430. A short 615 reads as 06:15. The date moves on a day after each add.

Offline: everything works without internet once the app has been opened online. When you're back online it picks up any update by itself.

Alarms: tap the clock next to the wake-up time to add both the wake-up and leave-home alarms through your "E-Gate Alarm" shortcut. Clock alarms have no date, so add them within 24 hours of waking up. Tap again to mark as not added.

Lock Screen widget: install the free Scriptable app, create a script named WakeUp and paste in the widget script (copy it below). Add a Scriptable widget to your Lock Screen and choose WakeUp. Tap Update widget at the bottom after each change. It shows a live countdown to your next wake-up, then to your bus.

All times are Dubai time (GMT+4). Duties disappear 1 hour after e-gate.
